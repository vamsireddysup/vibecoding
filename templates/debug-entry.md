## YYYY-MM-DD: one-line summary

**Symptom.** What was observed, quoting actual error text or signal values.

**Reproduction.** Exact command and conditions. Record the seed if intermittent.

**Hypothesis.** Stated so it could be shown wrong.

**Experiment.** What you did and what it showed. Keep disproved hypotheses.

**Root cause.** The specific line or decision, and why it produces the symptom.
Write "not yet established" rather than a plausible guess.

**Fix.** What changed, and why it addresses the cause rather than the symptom.

**What would have caught this earlier.** A check, assertion, lint rule or test.
