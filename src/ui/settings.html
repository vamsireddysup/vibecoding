Meeting Notes settings

AI provider

Summaries are generated with your own API key, billed to your own account. The key is sent only to the provider you choose, directly from your browser.

Anthropic
OpenAI-compatible

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works: OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio, OpenRouter, Azure, or your own gateway. Pointing this at a local model means a meeting never leaves your machine — not the audio, and not the transcript.

Local endpoints usually need no key — leave this blank.

Free text on purpose — model names change often, and differ per server. A wrong name comes back as a clear "not found" error.

Summaries

Added to the built-in instructions rather than replacing them, so the rules that stop the model inventing owners and deadlines stay in force.

Transcription

Speech is recognized on your own machine by Chrome. Audio is never uploaded, and transcription costs nothing.

Where your data lives

  • Transcripts and summaries are stored locally in this browser profile.
  • Your API key is stored in chrome.storage.local, unencrypted. Chrome gives extensions no secret store, so anyone with access to this computer profile could read it. Use a key you can revoke.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Summarize or ask a question.
